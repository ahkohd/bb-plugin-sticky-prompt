Keep the message you are reading a reply to pinned at the top of the chat,
so you always know what the agent is answering.

## What you get

- As soon as your message reaches the top of the chat, a two-line copy of it
  stays there.
- Your next message pushes it away as it comes up to the top.
- Images in your message show as small thumbnails.
- Click the pinned message to scroll back to it.
- Only messages you typed are pinned. A setting adds messages from agents,
  other threads and BB system notices.

## How it works

The plugin reads the thread's messages through BB's own API with your
session. It stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere.
